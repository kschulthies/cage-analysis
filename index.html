Why these weights fit SaaS

Administrative · 35%: privacy, cross-border processing, digital-services VAT, and market-entry requirements can affect the ability and cost of serving customers. Economic · 30%: price sensitivity and the large income gap make willingness to pay and packaging central. Cultural · 25%: language helps, while trust, sales relationships, support, and localization still shape adoption. Geographic · 10%: cloud delivery reduces physical shipping distance, so time zones, latency, and connectivity matter but receive less weight.

Initial country score = mean of the four ratings · Industry fit = Σ (rating × industry weight) ÷ 100

Scale: 1 = substantial distance or friction; 3 = mixed conditions that require adaptation; 5 = close fit or limited friction. Scores are analyst judgments, not measured probabilities.
